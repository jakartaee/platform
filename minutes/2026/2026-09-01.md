## Top of mind for Jared, James

* No new CCRs

* If you are working on a component spec update, make sure your **specification** includes the 1.1 or 2.0 of **EFSL** (Eclipse Foundation Specification License), not 1.0.  
  * If currently you have 1.0 move to 2.0  
  * If currently you have 1.1, you can stay at 1.1 or move to 2.0  
  * Don’t confuse this with **EPL** (Eclipse Public License) 2.0 for the **API** and **implementation** license

* [https://github.com/jakartaee/validation/issues/345](https://github.com/jakartaee/validation/issues/345) was brought up recently in slack  
  * Trying to find a home in the Jakarta ecosystem for this idea.  Validation may not be the right location.  Soliciting any ideas.  
  * REST has a default value concept for its \*Param annotations  
  * Could maybe standardize something in Jakarta Annotations and be used in multiple specifications (i.e. CDI, Data, Persistence, etc)?  
  * Jakarta Config also has a default mechanism.    
  * Annotations might be the right place to home the annotations, but other specs would need to define their use.

* New platform TCK issue: [\#2734](https://github.com/jakartaee/platform-tck/issues/2734)  
  * Intermittent failure  
  * Seems to be a fair challenge and can be fixed with a simple update to the test  
  * Sept 2 is the next Platform TCK call  
  * Scott also sent an email over to EJB since the EJB TCK is there for future use
